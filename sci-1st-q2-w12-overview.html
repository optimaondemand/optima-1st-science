Key Terms
Vocabulary Words

LIFE CYCLE

The stages a living thing goes through from the beginning of its life to the end, and then starting over again.
🌱
SPROUT
Tap to reveal definition
A tiny new plant that pushes up out of a seed and begins to grow leaves.
